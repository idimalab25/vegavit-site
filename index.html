VEGAVIT.

Software that turns raw data into clear answers

VEGAVIT EOOD is a Bulgarian software company based in Plovdiv. We design and run our own web services: AI-driven news processing, monitoring tools and cost-saving utilities for working with large language models.

Software projects

lambit2FA

lambit2FA — two-factor sign-in codes. The codes are kept in your phone, are synchronised between your own devices and reach nobody, us included, in readable form. Up to 300 codes. Free, no advertising.

Privacy policy

AI World Digest

ai-digest-1.com

An hourly digest of world artificial-intelligence news, published in ten languages. Sources are collected automatically, filtered for significance and summarised into short cards.

TokenDryer

tokendryer.com

A token economiser for large language models: it reduces the volume of text sent to an AI model, so the same work costs less.

In development for iOS, Android, macOS and Windows.

FMCheck

fmcheck.online

A cloud service for climate and cold-chain monitoring — for food producers, pharmaceuticals and medicine, biobanks and transport. Own low-cost sensors, tamper-evident records, alarms and calibration tracking. In development under the working name CoolBoxMonitor.

In development for iOS, Android, macOS and Windows.

MoreMoreIdea

—

An AI-driven generator of management decisions built on morphological vectors.

In development for iOS, Android, macOS and Windows.

Fish & Mushrooms

A group of native applications with a shared server. You keep a private log of your catches and finds — fishing, hunting, mushrooms, berries — and may share a spot with one person of your choosing. There is no public feed and no common map: locations are never disclosed to everyone.

In development for iOS, Android, macOS and Windows.
