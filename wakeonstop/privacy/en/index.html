WakeOnStop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026
Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short

WakeOnStop collects no information about you. No personal data, no anonymous data, no analytics, no crash reports.

Everything is calculated on your phone. Your location is never sent anywhere — not to us and not to anyone else. The app has no servers of its own.

Operator: VEGAVIT EOOD, UIC 207380781

2. What data the app stores

DataWhy
Marked stations and your own places, their minutes and favouritesTo alert you before them

All of this stays on your device only. We do not receive or see it.

3. Location

  • Location is used to calculate the time to your marked stations and alert you in time.
  • During a trip the app also follows your location in the background — otherwise it could not alert you while the screen is off.
  • Location is processed on the device only, is not kept after the trip and is never sent anywhere.

4. What the app does NOT do

  • does not send your location or routes anywhere, including to us;
  • does not contact our servers — there are none;
  • does not use analytics, counters, advertising or tracking libraries;
  • does not collect crash reports;
  • does not read your contacts, calendar or photos;
  • does not show ads;
  • does not share or sell data to third parties — there is nothing to share.

5. Going online

  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
  • New version check — only for subscribers to station list updates, at most once a day: the app asks Apple for the number of the latest version in the App Store. The request contains only the app identifier and the store country, nothing about you.
  • The map on screen is Apple Maps and is shown under Apple's terms.

6. Apple Watch

Data for the watch (nearest station, distance, time) is sent directly from your iPhone to your watch.

7. Device permissions

  • Location "Always" — to follow the trip while the screen is off.
  • Alarms — to alert you at the station with vibration and sound.
  • Notifications — trip messages and the end of the free period.

8. Children

The app collects no data about anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

All data stays on your device. We do not have it, so there is no need — and no technical way — to ask us for access, correction or deletion. You can delete everything by deleting the app from your device.

Your rights under the GDPR (EU), CCPA (California) and Federal Law 152-FZ (Russia) are respected by design: no data that could identify a person ever exists on our side.

10. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date.
